Interrogating the Data: Avoiding the Circularity Trap

The Initial Data Problem

  • !
    Available sample workbooks showed rigid translation: temporal σ = 0.0143 for all landmarks. Zero real arm swing or limb flexion.
  • !
    The Circularity Trap: Writing heuristic rules to score data and then training ML to predict that heuristic proves nothing.

The Physics-Based Solution

  • ✓
    Latent Physics Formulation: True biomechanical qualities (q) drive a 3D articulated kinematic model (Drillis & Contini segment ratios + IK).
  • ✓
    Camera projection model with focal perspective, randomized sensor noise, and dropout creates realistic corrupted 2D observations.

Kinematic Normalization

Signal Processing & Gait Phase Detection

Scale & Distance Invariance

As a walker approaches the camera, their vertical scale expands up to 7×, dwarfing lateral hip and shoulder sway (magnitude ~0.2 torso lengths).

  • 1
    Pelvis Origin & Torso Normalization: Scores remain identical whether recorded at 3 meters or 8 meters.
  • 2
    Zero-Phase Butterworth High-Pass (0.45 Hz): Eliminates distance drift without distorting oscillation timing.

Hilbert Instantaneous Gait Phase

Standard peak-picking produces phantom strikes (detecting 27 steps on a 6s clip).

  • θ
    Hilbert Phase Transform: Constructs analytic signal z(t) = s(t) + iH[s(t)] for continuous cycle tracking.
  • ✓
    Guarantees exactly one stance event per step cycle with sub-frame cadence accuracy.

Modeling Strategy

12 Specialized Regressors vs. Black-Box DL

Why Tabular ML over End-to-End Deep Learning?

  • 1
    Clinical & Biomechanical Explainability: Coaches and models need to know the exact physical fault (e.g. shoulder tilt 36.8° vs ideal 2.1°), not an uninterpretable latent vector.
  • 2
    Sample Efficiency: On 1,500 clips with pre-extracted kinematic signals, tree ensembles outperform heavy neural networks (Grinsztajn et al., 2022).

Model Selection & Blending

Each criterion independently selects its best model based strictly on out-of-fold cross-validation MAE:

Histogram Gradient Boosting (HGB): Best for nonlinear interactions (Balance, Head Position, Stride Length).
Ridge + Gradient Boost Blend: Optimal for smooth, highly correlated human qualities (Confidence, Rhythm, Symmetry).

What Real Video Revealed: The "New Scores" Story

Real-World Distribution Shift

Testing against real smartphone recordings uncovered differences synthetic models hid:

  • !
    Detector Noise Variance: MediaPipe jitter on moving mobile cameras differs from idealized synthetic tracks.
  • !
    MAE Shift (0.902 → 1.317): The error metric increased because the evaluation tested genuine out-of-distribution stress.

The Transparent Safeguards

  • ✓
